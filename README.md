### What Cowpin does

- **Bring your whole library** — [import from Pinboard](https://www.cowpin.com/docs/getting-started/importing-from-pinboard) with every tag kept, or from Pocket, Instapaper, Readwise, Karakeep, wallabag, Reddit, X and browser exports.
- **Find anything again** — full-text search over the archived text of every page you save, so a dead link never takes the content with it.
- **Keep your tools** — a [Pinboard-compatible API](https://www.cowpin.com/docs/getting-started/api), so existing Pinboard scripts and apps keep working.
- **Save from anywhere** — browser extension, bookmarklet and save-by-email.
- **Ask your own library** — AI answers with citations, summaries and smart search over what *you* saved.
